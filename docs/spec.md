what the app is all about?

This app helps students can record everyday spending and see where the money goes.so it's all about how the students can be tracking his/her expenditures day by day. it also warns students in case they go over they spending limit. it works on the browser and keeps everything in the student's own device,so no account or internet is needed.

what does on transation look like?
every transaction saved by the app has seven pieces of information:
id; unique label for the transaction,it's created by the app automatically

Description; A short piece of text saying what money was spent on for example transport and all that.

amount; a number ssaying how much was spent for example 12.50

category; a word or short phrases saying what kind of spending it was.for example "Food"

date; a day the money was spent on.

createdAt; the exact time the transaction was first saved

updatedAt; the exact time the transaction was last changed
